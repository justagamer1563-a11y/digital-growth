Search engines prioritize natural, readable content. - **"SEO is a one-time task."** Search algorithms change constantly, so SEO requires ongoing attention. - **"Paid ads improve your organic ranking."** Google Ads and organic SEO rankings are separate systems — paying for ads doesn't boost your unpaid search results. ## Final Thoughts SEO can feel overwhelming at first, but at its heart, it's about one simple goal: making your website genuinely helpful and easy for both people and search engines to understand.
